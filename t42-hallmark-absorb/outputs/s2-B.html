How we roast

01

Source

We buy in small lots directly from importers who share farm-level data — altitude, varietal, process, harvest date.

02

Cup

Every lot is cupped blind against three roast profiles before we commit to a full batch size.

03

Roast

12kg batches on a refurbished 1985 Probat, gas-fed drum, adjusted by hand through every stage of the curve.

04

Rest

Roasted beans rest 5 to 10 days so CO2 can escape before we bag a single order.

05

Ship

Bagged and shipped within 48 hours of roast — never before, no exceptions.

The Studio

No café. Just the drum, the beans, and the smell of a Tuesday roast.

Emberline is a working roastery, not a coffee shop — there's no espresso bar, no seating, no oat milk menu. What you'll find is a 1985 Probat running four mornings a week and bags going out the door the same day they're filled.

Retail pickup is by appointment. Email ahead and we'll have your order bagged and dated when you arrive.

Questions

Before you order

Do you have a café?

No — Emberline is a roasting studio, not a coffee shop. We sell beans, not lattes.

How fresh is the coffee when it ships?

We ship within 48 hours of roast, and the roast date is printed on every bag — no batch codes to decode.

Do you offer wholesale?

Yes, for cafés and offices ordering 5kg or more per month. Email wholesale@emberlinecoffee.com to start a conversation.

Can I taste before I subscribe?

We hold a public cupping the first Saturday of every month at 10am at the studio. No RSVP needed, just show up.

Good coffee is a decision made hundreds of times per batch.

One email a month when we drop a new lot. No spam, no filler.
